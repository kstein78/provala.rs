## A lake that breathes with the Danube

No river flows into Provala. It is fed by **rain, groundwater and the Danube**, and loses water by evaporation and seepage ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala)). The lake is linked to the river underground, so **its level rises and falls with the Danube**, at most about a metre apart ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/), [Turizmopedija](https://www.turizmopedija.com/jezero-provala/)).

## What the water is like

A test in **1995** rated the water **first class**, slightly alkaline (pH 8.5–8.6), with 7.1–10.1 mg/l of dissolved oxygen ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala)). In hot summers algae grow more and the water becomes less clear ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala)). "The water in the lake is clean," says Stevan Adamović of the angling club "Šaran" ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)).

We have not found more recent public water-quality data. If you have any, please write to us.

## Mind the depth

The first ten metres or so from the shore are shallow ([Turizmopedija](https://www.turizmopedija.com/jezero-provala/)); then the bottom drops quickly, down to 19 m. There is no lifeguard ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/)).
