The name says it all: *provala* means a breach. The lake formed when the Danube, in a great flood, **broke through the embankment** near Vajska. When the river withdrew, water stayed in the hollow ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/), [Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala)).

## 1924 or 1926?

Sources disagree on the year. The Vojvodina Tourist Organisation and Turizmopedija give **1926** ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/), [Turizmopedija](https://www.turizmopedija.com/jezero-provala/)). Turistički klub and Stevan Adamović, president of the angling club "Šaran" from Vajska, give **1924**, with 1926 and 1965 as later big floods ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala), [Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). According to Adamović there was a marsh here before, and the lake took its present shape after the 1965 flood ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)).

Turistički klub also notes that the Danube reached 82 m above sea level then, 3 m above the floodplain, and that the embankment was strengthened after 1965 ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala)).

## Size

The lake is about 390 m long and 180 m wide, about 4.2 ha in area ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala), [Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). The greatest depth measured today is 19 m; according to Adamović, 36 m was once measured ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). The average depth is given as about 7 m, and most of the lake is 2–5 m deep ([Turistički klub](https://www.turistickiklub.com/sadrzaj/jezero-provala), [Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/)).
