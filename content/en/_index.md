## Where Provala is

The lake lies about a kilometre from the village of Vajska in Bač municipality, 14 km from Bač ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/)). By car it is about 78 km from Novi Sad, 160 km from Belgrade and about an hour from Ilok in Croatia.

## Why people come

- **Swimming:** a sandy beach with parasols, sun loungers, changing cabins and showers ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/)). There is no lifeguard.
- **Fishing:** the lake is connected to the Danube and full of fish: carp, catfish, pike, zander, grass carp and bass ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)).
- **Quiet and nature:** the wood around the beach gives shade, and nearby are the Plavna hunting ground, the Bač fishpond and the Danube.

The village of Vajska, its feasts and trips nearby are on our sister site [vajska.rs](https://vajska.rs/en/).
