"Bački dvor" is a motel and restaurant on the shore of Lake Provala, in a building made to look like a small castle with towers. It has a restaurant, a café-bar, a large terrace facing the lake, a hall for weddings and celebrations, and eight double rooms with private bathrooms ([Turizmopedija](https://www.turizmopedija.com/jezero-provala/), [hranaipice.net](https://www.hranaipice.net/motel-restoran-backi-dvor.html)).

## Restaurant

Konstantin Stein had lunch here on 10 November 2026: the portions are huge and the choice is interesting, for example "Šnicla de la Provala". The menu is on the [restaurant's website](http://www.backidvor.rs/).

{{< photos "backi-dvor-dinner backi-dvor-emblem" >}}

## Opening hours and payment

According to the notice on the door on 10 November 2026, the restaurant is open Monday to Friday 10:00–22:00 and Saturday and Sunday 8:00–22:00. The bill can be paid only in dinars or by card. For rooms and celebrations, call.
