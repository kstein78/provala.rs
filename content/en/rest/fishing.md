## What you can catch

Provala holds **carp, pike, zander, catfish, grass carp, silver carp, bass and bream** ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). Stevan Adamović, president of the angling club "Šaran" in Vajska, believes Provala may be the richest bass lake in Serbia; bass were first stocked in the 1980s ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). Grass carp were released to keep the water plants in check and are caught mostly in September and October ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)).

## Records

A catfish of 86 kg and 2.6 m, a 27 kg carp, pike of 8–9 kg, catfish of 30–40 kg and Prussian carp of 2–3 kg ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). On sunny weekends dozens of anglers line the shore, and competitions are held here ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/), [Turizmopedija](https://www.turizmopedija.com/jezero-provala/)).

## The Šaran club

The lake is stocked and looked after by the **Šaran fishing club of Vajska** ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)). Its members include Blaženka and Miodrag “Dragan” Davidović, Serbia's angling champions in 1999 and 2000; the municipality of Bač gave them its October Award for their work with the club ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).

## Before you cast

- The water is managed by the **Fishing Association of Serbia** ([Bač vesti](https://bacvesti.com/2024/04/03/jezero-provala-kraj-vajske-po-mnogo-cemu-specificno/)). You need a valid Serbian fishing licence; ask the "Šaran" club in Vajska or on site where to buy one and which rules apply at Provala.
- In summer the beach is busy: fish away from the swimming area.
