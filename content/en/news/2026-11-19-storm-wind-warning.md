On Thursday, 19 November, at 9:10 the Republic Hydrometeorological Service of Serbia (RHMZ) issued an urgent weather warning and sent emergency SMS messages to people. **This evening**, as a cold front passes, **storm and hurricane-force gusts of westerly and north-westerly wind of 80 to 110 km/h** are expected in Vojvodina, Belgrade and western and central Serbia. The **red** alert ("particularly dangerous weather that can cause great material damage") covers Belgrade, Srem, Banat and **Bačka**.

Provala is ringed by poplars and willows on the plain by the Danube, where nothing stops a westerly wind. In wind like this, branches and whole trees come down on the shore, the roads and the car park.

The RHMZ forecast says it turns warm again on Friday (16–20 °C), and from Saturday temperatures will gradually fall, with occasional rain.

## Anglers and visitors

- **Do not come to the lake tonight**. If you are already on the shore, leave before dark.
- **Put away fishing gear**, tarpaulins and umbrellas.
- **Do not stay in a tent or caravan**, and do not leave your car under trees.

## Weekend-house owners

- Before dark, **close windows and shutters**, bring in or tie down furniture, barbecues, bins and anything the wind can pick up.
- **Do not light fires** or burn leaves: in strong wind a spark carries far.
- If the power goes: a torch, a charged phone and power bank, and a supply of water — without electricity, pumps and hydrophores stop.

## During the storm

- Do not drive through the forest unless you must. **Do not stand under trees** or poles, **do not touch fallen wires**, and report them.

## Numbers

- **112** — emergency services; **192** — police, **193** — firefighters, **194** — ambulance
- **Power cuts and fallen wires**: Elektrodistribucija Srbije, free number [0800 360 300](tel:0800360300)
